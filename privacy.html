Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Applies to Bubble Dodger on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Android

The short version
  • Bubble Dodger has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • We (CobGames) do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information from anyone who plays, whatever their age.
  • Your settings, scores and progress are saved on your device. Apple and Google services you already use (Game Center, iCloud, Android backup, in-app purchases) may handle some game data under their own privacy policies, as explained below.

Who we are

Bubble Dodger is made by CobGames, the independent studio of Corey Bovalina ("we", "us"). For anything about privacy, email bubbledodger.help@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

To work, the game keeps a small amount of information in its own storage on your device. It never leaves the device except through the platform services described in the next section.

WhatWhy
Music, sound and control settingsTo remember how you like to play
Top-10 high scores for each difficulty (score, difficulty, date)To show your high score tables and "new best" moments
Sand dollars, fish and trails you own, the fish you've chosen, your custom fish coloursTo keep your Shop and Fish Creator progress
Whether you've seen the tutorial; whether the Full Game is unlockedTo skip the tutorial next time and open unlocked content straight away

The game has no sign-up, never asks for a name, email, age, photo, location or contacts, and has no chat or sharing features.

Platform services the game uses

These services belong to Apple or Google. You (or a parent) choose whether to use them in your device settings. We don't receive the data they handle.

Game Center (Apple devices)

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends the final score of each run to Apple's Game Center leaderboard for Bubble Dodger. Your Game Center nickname and score may be visible to other players on that leaderboard, depending on your Game Center settings. Apple operates Game Center under the Apple Privacy Policy. Parents can limit or turn off Game Center in Screen Time. The game works fully without it.

iCloud (iPhone and iPad)

Your high score tables are also saved to your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage) so they can appear on your other devices. That data is stored in your iCloud account by Apple. We cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Android Auto Backup (Android)

If backup is turned on for your device, Android includes the game's saved settings and progress in your device backup to your Google account, so they come back when you reinstall or set up a new phone. Google stores the backup under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it. You can manage backup in your device's Settings.

In-app purchase (Apple and Google)

The optional Full Game unlock is sold and processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The game only checks, on your device, whether the unlock is owned. We never receive your payment details, name or email, and we don't run any server that stores purchases. Store purchases are covered by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No advertising of any kind, and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, attribution or other third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information. We don't hold any to share.
  • No links out of the game to websites or social media.

Children's privacy

Bubble Dodger is designed to be played by children as well as adults, and it is built so that no personal information is collected from anyone.

  • COPPA (United States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the game collects none, there is nothing for which we need to seek verifiable parental consent.
  • GDPR and UK GDPR, including the rules for children's data ("GDPR-K") and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. We do not process children's personal data. Game data stays on the device or in the family's own Apple or Google account. There is no profiling, no geolocation and no nudging towards sharing data.
  • Purchases. The purchase screen sits behind a parental gate: a grown-up has to tap three numbers that are spelled out in words. Apple's Ask to Buy and Google's Family Link purchase approvals are supported, and the game shows "Waiting for a grown-up to approve it" while a request is pending.

If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, for example by emailing us, contact bubbledodger.help@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

If you email us

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, don't add you to any mailing list, and delete support emails when they're no longer needed to help you (at most 12 months). Please don't include a child's personal details in support emails.

Your choices and rights

Everything the game saves is under your control:

  • Delete it: uninstall the app, or on Android clear its storage in Settings → Apps. That removes everything the game saved on the device.
  • iCloud copy of scores: manage or delete it through your iCloud settings.
  • Game Center scores: managed by Apple through your Game Center account.
  • Android backup: managed through your Google account's backup settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California privacy law), you have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data from the game, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you are always welcome to ask, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads the Nunito typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address (see the Google Privacy Policy). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles data (for example, adding a new platform service), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released, and update the store privacy labels to match. We will never add advertising or sell data.
